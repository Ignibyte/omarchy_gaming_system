## What happened

The separate Usurper workspace advanced to unadmitted rules, state, and
cartridge v15. It now stores exact v0.20e Level 10 editor records 90–99,
supports draw-free switching across levels one through ten, preserves the
source-order `Random(100)` rejection loop, and initializes normally reachable
Level 10 monsters at strength 20, defence 10, and 60 HP. Existing combat,
healing, spell, class-special, reward, retreat, and Gnoll poison paths remain
owned by the same deterministic reducer.

The first v15 provider profile exposed an expected integration effect: the
stronger deterministic Level 10 encounter killed the development character,
so the lower-level continuation submitted a phase-invalid command and received
a bounded 422. An exact trace reconciled the profile to the post-command phase,
after which the fifteen-case provider corpus passed twice across restart.

The complete external suite passed strict formatting/Clippy/rustdoc, all 83
Rust tests, immutable source/provenance and privacy checks, seventeen signed
screens, unique-label and activation-repeat regressions, and provider-backed
trusted-QML play through Level 10 combat. The full 24-stage platform gate
passed. The development window is left open on workspace 8; because the
desktop was locked, no input was sent into its password prompt. A sealed
security review reported zero findings and zero deferred candidates. OpenWiki
updated both relevant pages while reporting their pre-existing Claims debt.
