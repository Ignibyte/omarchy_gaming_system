## What happened

The separate Usurper workspace advanced to unadmitted rules, state, and
cartridge v13. It stores the exact Level 8 records 70–79, permits draw-free
switching across levels one through eight, preserves every rejected
`Random(80)` draw until records 71–79 are selected, and initializes Level 8
combat at strength 18, defence 9, and 54 HP. Existing attack, retreat, potion,
spell, class-special, reward, and Gnoll poison behavior remains inside the same
deterministic provider-owned reducer.

The final external suite passed 71 Rust tests plus Clippy, rustdoc, immutable
upstream/provenance and privacy checks, and all seventeen signed screens. The
live provider passed the fixed fifteen-case TLS, authentication, replay, fault,
callback, and reconciliation corpus twice across restart. A complete security
diff scan reported zero findings. The full platform gate was green, and the
signed Level 8 combat fixture visibly rendered a Sandworm at 54 HP through
production trusted QML on workspace 8. OpenWiki and the hand-maintained
architecture were reconciled before archival.

## Effectiveness

Effective. Recalled legacy-boundary and discarded-RNG rules preserved record 70
without making it normally reachable, and full-profile replay proved Level 8
against prior combat features twice across restart. The harness review finished
applying the existing Cargo metadata rule to all affected executables. Exact
data/trace tests, zero-finding security review, the complete platform gate, and
the visible workspace-8 render jointly prove a source-linked deterministic
eight-level slice without expanding platform gameplay authority. The preview
feedback also makes the next product gap concrete: a provider-backed local-play
shell, distinct from the inert signed-fixture renderer.
