## What happened

The separate Usurper workspace advanced to unadmitted rules, state, and
cartridge v17. It stores the exact v0.20e Level 12 editor records 110–119,
switches draw-free across levels one through twelve, preserves source-order
`Random(120)` rejection work, normally selects only records 111–119, and
initializes the reachable band at strength 20, defence 10, and 60 HP. Existing
attack, retreat, healing, spell, class-special, reward, and Gnoll poison paths
remain inside the same provider-owned deterministic reducer.

The external view/schema adds one bounded `option_l`, and the signed cartridge
binds it to exactly one Level 12 action. All seventeen views carry the strict
field, the signed presentation retains 117 unique nodes, and no Level 13,
navigation twin, provider protocol, trusted renderer vocabulary, platform
gameplay rule, database, or migration was added.

The complete external suite passed 92 Rust tests, strict lint/docs,
source/provenance validation, five real-input Qt cases, seventeen signed-screen
cases, and provider-backed local play. The fifteen-case live provider corpus
passed twice across restart on disposable PostgreSQL 18. The final canonical
platform diff gate passed all 24 stages in an isolated network namespace and
bound state hash
`11143f81e11935483982631dda60018b3cee46b62def1d736a4a3b69a3c72f4a`.
OpenWiki reconciled the quickstart and cartridge architecture, and the second
post-architecture finalization completed cleanly at the same state. One current
v17 local-play window remains mapped on workspace 8 with exactly one live entry
action.
