## What happened

The adjacent Usurper repository became a buildable GPL-2.0-or-later Rust
workspace rather than a mock presentation. Its pure rules engine now covers a
source-linked deterministic first BBS day, its provider adapter consumes only
the packaged public Provider SDK/starter, and its independently signed inert
cartridge renders eleven screens through the platform-owned QML surface. The
provider ran the fixed fifteen-case TLS/PostgreSQL conformance corpus twice
across restart and delivered its persistent-session `TurnReady` callback.

Two platform contracts that had been described as reusable were still coupled
to pilot games. The conformance runner embedded Relay Forge's commands and
terminal outcome, while authenticated provider projection required Door
Legends' exact five-string view. Both were generalized at their intended public
boundaries without changing provider protocol v1, the fixed security/fault
corpus, authentication, replay, callback, authority, or signed presentation
checks. Direct canonical Pascal inspection also corrected three early port
assumptions before they became compatibility claims.

The result remains deliberately development-only: no Usurper rule, route,
migration, registration, admission, deployment, publication, publisher QML,
or compiled fallback entered the platform repository.

## Prevention rules captured

| ID | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `PR-omarchy-gaming-system-parameterize-gameplay-without-weakening-provider-security-corpus-001` | Keep transport, authentication, replay, fault, callback, reconciliation, receipt, and case identities fixed; parameterize only the bounded game launch/command sequence and expected lifecycle. | A reusable conformance harness must accept different game semantics without silently reducing its security proof. |
| `PR-omarchy-gaming-system-validate-provider-views-by-public-bounds-not-pilot-vocabulary-001` | At the provider projection boundary, enforce the public safe-payload and byte contracts; leave exact game fields to the authenticated cartridge schema. | The platform must not become a second copy of an external game's presentation vocabulary. |
| `PR-omarchy-gaming-system-prove-legacy-branch-scope-before-porting-behavior-001` | Trace a legacy statement through its enclosing conditions and canonical data declaration before translating it; record deliberate oddities in compatibility tests. | Proximity in procedural source is not evidence that two statements apply to the same actor or path. |

## Effectiveness

Effective. The recalled source-rights and state-topology rules kept the first
slice on the authenticated v0.20e baseline, prevented historical art or
ambiguous infrastructure from entering the cartridge, and constrained the
session starter claim to the solo milestone. The new conformance and view rules
were proven by the unchanged Relay Forge tests, Usurper's restart run, hostile
payload tests, and the complete local delivery gate.
